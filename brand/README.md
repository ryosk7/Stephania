# Stephania brand assets

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `stephania-mark.png` (1024 px) and `stephania-mark-{512,256,128,64,32}.png` | The mark on a transparent background: the leaf lens with the red live light. |
| `stephania-lockup-light.png` / `stephania-lockup-dark.png` | Mark and wordmark for light and dark backgrounds. |
| `stephania-app-icon.png` | The full-bleed 1024 px iOS app icon, no alpha (`apps/mobile/assets/icon.png`). |

The wordmark is set in Inter Display SemiBold (SIL Open Font License). Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width, and do not recolour, stretch or add effects to it. The PNGs are reduced to 256-colour palettes; the uncompressed artwork is not in the repository.
