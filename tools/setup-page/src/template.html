Stephania

Create the setup secret that makes your phone the owner of a Stephania device. Everything happens in this browser: the page makes no network connections and works offline.

This browser does not provide secure random numbers and hashing here. Open the page again over HTTPS or as a file:// URL.

  1. Download stephania-setup.txt
  2. Copy it to the top level of the boot (FAT32) partition of the microSD card with the OS.
  3. Insert the card into the Stephania device and power it on. The first boot imports the file and deletes it from the card.
  4. Scan the QR code with the Stephania Connect app to register as the owner over Bluetooth.
  5. When registration is done, and close this tab.

The QR code and the file are the ownership of the Stephania device. Do not show them to anyone or put them in shared folders or chats, and do not hand the card to anyone before registration. A device that already has an owner ignores and deletes the file without changing its secret.

Page integrity: compare this file's SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS published alongside it.
